# Approach — Masked Discourse Sequence Recovery

Time spent: about 5 hours.

## Submission text (paste into the Eris form)

I treated the task as parent recovery by graph parsing plus a learned 5 class type sequence decoder, since parents are nearly determined by topology while types carry 80 percent of the metric.

Parents: the thread_view card list is a DFS preorder of the neighborhood, so a masked node's parent is the nearest preceding card at depth d minus 1; depth 1 nodes attach to the post card (d equals minus 1) when present, else ROOT. This recovers 99.9 percent of train parents.

Types: I train 3 diverse per node emission families on a shared 5 fold split (stratified by anchor class and route length): (1) LightGBM multiclass on 183 dense structural and context features (position, depth, gaps, parent kind and visible parent type, visible child, descendant, sibling and neighborhood type counts, thread profile flags, title statistics, hashed character trigram counts of title and forum), (2) multinomial logistic regression on stateless hashed title and forum ngram features (word 1 to 2 grams and character 3 to 5 grams for the title, character 2 to 5 grams for the forum; pure feature hashing with no idf weighting and no corpus vocabulary) stacked with 90 standardized dense features, and (3) torch BiGRU and MLP joint route taggers over the 3 to 4 route positions with embeddings for parent type, role and depth plus hashed character ngram embedding bags for title and forum, seed averaged. Class balanced weighting is the decisive lever everywhere because the metric is macro F1 heavy.

The 3 families' out of fold probabilities are blended with log linear weights selected on out of fold data against the exact competition metric, then a stacked logistic regression meta model is trained on family log probabilities, the blend, neighbor node blend probabilities, structural features and the hashed text features, with the same fold protocol (the meta model for fold f never sees fold f rows). A dedicated anchor position specialist of the same form is trained on the route final nodes and geometrically mixed into the anchor position only when it improves out of fold score. Routes are decoded with position conditioned label transition tables estimated from the training targets, using max marginal or Viterbi decoding, mode selected on out of fold score. All hyperparameter choices happen inside the script on training data only.

What worked: the DFS parent rule; class balanced weighting (+1.9 macro F1); the stacked meta model with neighbor probabilities and ratio features (+11 over the best single family, from 53.1 to 64.6 out of fold) letting a linear stacker do joint route reasoning; stateless feature hashing for text, which both avoids corpus level statistics and generalizes better to the 45 percent of test forums unseen in train; posterior max marginal decoding for the sharp stacked probabilities. What did not work: tfidf weighting (replaced by hashing, which scored 3 points higher); free per role per class multiplier tuning against the metric overfit badly (negative cross validated gain, rejected by a nested half split check); the anchor specialist was rejected on out of fold score once the meta model included neighbor context; per role models for the linear family; test distribution calibration was never used.

Local validation: out of fold on the 1250 training rows with an exact reimplementation of the official metric (verified: perfect submission scores 100, best constant route scores 13.48 versus the published 13.46; the stacking gain was audited by reproducing the fold protocol bit for bit and checking uniform gains across all 5 folds). Final out of fold score 64.63 (TypeMacroF1 0.588, AnchorMacroF1 0.584, TypeScore 0.635, OrderedScore 0.817, ParentScore 0.999) versus published baselines 13.46 constant, 22.82 topology parent, 38.96 local window transition, 41.57 public feature ensemble.

Runtime: about 10 minutes on a 16 core CPU box end to end (budget manager caps training inside the grading limit and a fallback always writes a valid submission). The script follows the platform contract: python3 solution.py public_dir submission_out.
